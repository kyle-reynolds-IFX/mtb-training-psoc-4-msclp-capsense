# PSOC™ 4 CAPSENSE™ training
This training introduces the 5th generation Multi-Sense Converter with Low-Power (MSCLP) available on the PSOC™ 4000T and PSOC™ 4100T Plus. It covers updates provided by the 5th generation MSCLP block, the capacitive sensing technology, and goes through two hands on labs for tuning capacitive sensors and the low power tuning procedure. 


## Device family
* [PSOC™ 4](https://www.infineon.com/products/microcontroller/32-bit-psoc-arm-cortex/psoc-4-mcu)
